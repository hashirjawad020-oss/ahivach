SCHOOL & COMMUNITY OUTREACH PROGRAMME

What the outreach layer covers

  • Recognising common venomous species without needing to handle or kill the snake
  • Safe footwear and torch use when walking at night or in fields
  • What to do — and what never to do — in the first minutes after a bite
  • When and how to use the WhatsApp / IVR emergency channel
  • Seasonal snake-movement awareness tied to monsoon and harvest cycles
HOUSEHOLD PREVENTION CHECKLIST
  • Use a torch and wear closed footwear when walking outdoors after dark
  • Keep the area around the home clear of grain stores, brush and debris where snakes shelter
  • Sleep on a raised bed or use a tucked-in mosquito net
  • Never handle a snake, even if it appears dead or injured
  • Store the AHIVACH helpline number and emergency card somewhere accessible
