DO

  • Keep the person calm and still
  • Keep the bitten limb below heart level
  • Remove rings, watches, tight clothing near the bite
  • Note the time of the bite
  • Get to a hospital that stocks polyvalent antivenom (ASV) immediately

DO NOT

  • Do not cut the wound
  • Do not apply a tourniquet
  • Do not try to suck out venom
  • Do not apply ice
  • Do not give food, alcohol, or traditional remedies
